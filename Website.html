Chinese cuisine

Chinese cuisine is one of the world's oldest and most diverse culinary traditions, shaped by thousands of years of regional geography, cultural philosophy, and history.At its core, a traditional Chinese meal emphasizes a harmonious balance between fan (grains and starches like rice or noodles) and cai (accompanying vegetable and meat dishes), as well as the philosophical balance of flavors, colors, and textures.
